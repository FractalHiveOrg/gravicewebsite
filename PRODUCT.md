## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion — SEO-ready marketing site for gravicesolutions.com, deployable on Vercel.

## Users

Primary: decision-makers and operators at banks, NBFCs, and growing institutional businesses (relationship managers, credit/risk/compliance officers, operations and finance leads) evaluating software for high-stakes, manual workflows. Secondary: enterprise procurement and IT stakeholders in India and the Middle East.

## Product Purpose

Gravice builds fintech software that brings order to slow, manual, high-stakes work so enterprises and growing businesses can move faster with less risk. Success is trusted evaluation and demo bookings from institutional buyers.

## Positioning

Assurance-first platforms for modern banking — strengthening governance without disrupting core systems. Gravice sits above LOS, CMS, ERP, and core banking (formerly Fractal-Hive Solutions Private Limited). Live products: ElevateRM (relationship management), V-Track (valuation assurance; patent pending), and NICO (intelligent / agentic automation for document-heavy processes).

## Operating Context

Buyers research on desktop and mobile marketing pages, compare products by role, review trust/security posture, and book a 30-minute demo. Domain language includes collateral revaluation, relationship management, NBFCs, and institutional operations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Marketing site for gravicesolutions.com; Phase 1 ships the Home experience from Figma Website frames.
- Products named and described in brand copy: ElevateRM, V-Track, NICO.
- Demo booking in Phase 1 is a form UI with placeholder submission (no CRM wired yet).
- Insights and customer-story metrics may be placeholders until real content replaces them; do not invent commercial claims beyond Figma copy.
- Undecided: CMS, analytics vendor, production demo CRM.

- Visual identity from Figma “Gravice — Brand Identity (P0)”: Deep Space `#0E1B2C`, Gravice Navy `#1F3A5F`, Lattice Purple `#61149A`, Pull Orange `#F6682E` (<10% of layout), Paper `#F7F5F0`, Slate `#5B6B80`.

- Symbol: convex Gravity Mirror (9×9 lattice on a dome; Pull Orange core). Do not stretch, recolour core, rotate, or add glows/shadows/gradients to the logo.
- Tagline/positioning line: “Structure, at the centre.”
- Audience tone: professional, calm, tech-forward class for bankers and large enterprises.
- Binding design reference: Figma Website page (Home Desktop `77:295` and Mobile `82:496`).

## Product Principles

1. Earn institutional trust before cleverness — clarity, restraint, and accuracy over hype.
2. One idea across products: structure for complex work.
3. Speak in the buyer’s operating language (roles, collateral, compliance), not generic SaaS slogans.
4. Brand form follows the Gravity Mirror: centre of attraction, calm periphery, orange only where it pulls action.
5. Do not fabricate proof; placeholders stay honest until replaced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text ≥4.5:1 and large text/graphics ≥3:1 (WCAG AA) per brand Colour page. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for interactive mirror tracking.
